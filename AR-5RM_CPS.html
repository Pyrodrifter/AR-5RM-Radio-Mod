Satellite RX

Calculate up to 12 passes over the next 48 hours with a peak elevation of at least 10°. Enter your listening location and the FM downlink you want to monitor.

Get the ISS TLE from CelesTrak and check the ISS radio status. A pass tells you when the satellite is in view.

The first upload backs up and replaces Band Sweep. Set the radio’s UTC clock with * before tracking.


  

  

    
    
    
  




  
Radio apps

  
Radio Watch scan lists

  
Enter up to six named lists with up to 32 saved channels each. Use the channel numbers from the CPS table.

  
  
  
The first upload backs up and replaces Spectrum. Later uploads update the Radio Watch lists.

  
On the radio, open RADIO WATCH, choose a list, and press MENU. Press # for Recent Activity, then MENU on a hit to listen.

  

  

    
    
    
  




  
Radio apps

  
Text drafts

  
Enter one message per line. Save up to eight drafts of 50 plain keyboard characters each.

  
  
  
Read from the radio to edit its current list. Upload replaces the list, updates TEXT if needed, and saves a backup before writing.

  
On the radio, open TEXT and hold *. Use Up/Down to preview drafts, MENU to load one, and MENU again when ready to send.
