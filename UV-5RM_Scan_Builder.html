Load it onto the radio

  1. Connect the radio. In CHIRP, choose Radio > Download from radio... (Baofeng, 5RM).
  2. Choose File > Import from file... and pick the CSV you downloaded from this page.
  3. Choose Radio > Upload to radio...
  4. Download from the radio once more and confirm the Skip column shows S on channels you unticked.
  5. On the radio, start a scan while in channel mode.
